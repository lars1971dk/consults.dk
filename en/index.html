About CONSULTS

Freelance automation development from Herning, Denmark

Behind CONSULTS is Lars Anker Trankjær, self-employed in Herning since 2003. CONSULTS ApS was established as an independent company in 2024 with a focus on freelance automation development: PLC software to begin with, today also HMI, SCADA and robot integration.

Work is carried out for machine builders and manufacturing companies, either as extra capacity on large projects or as the specialist who solves a specific task. The principle is the same every time: solutions that are robust in operation, understandable for operators and maintainable for those who come after.

"At CONSULTS there are no project managers in between. You speak with the person who writes the code."

Questions & answers

What customers ask most often

Short, honest answers. If your question is not here, just call, it is the fastest way.

Which PLC platforms do you work with?+

Siemens and Beckhoff, among others. If you use a different platform, just ask. The initial review is free, and we will tell you honestly whether it is something we can help with.

Can you take over code written by others?+

Yes. A large part of the work is precisely existing plants where the original supplier is no longer available or where documentation is missing. We review the code before building on it.

What does it cost?+

It depends on the scope of the task. You always receive a concrete proposal with scope and timeline before we start, so both parties know what has been agreed. The first review costs nothing.

What does a typical project look like?+

Four steps: a no-obligation review of the task, a clear proposal with scope and timeline, development with ongoing status and testing, and finally commissioning with documentation and support after go-live.

What is the difference between using a freelancer and an agency?+

At CONSULTS there are no project managers in between. You speak directly with the person who writes the code, from the first review until the plant is running. That means shorter decision paths and no agency overhead.
